Our story

Five Decades of Trust

Founded in 1970, Madhava Medicals Clinic began as a small neighbourhood pharmacy in Chittoor, Andhra Pradesh. Over five decades, we have grown into a comprehensive healthcare destination — combining a full-service medical shop with expert clinical consultations.

Our commitment has never wavered: to provide honest, affordable, and compassionate healthcare to every family that walks through our doors.
